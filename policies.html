Shop Policies

Please review our booking guidelines and cancellation policy prior to your appointment.

📌 Deposit & Booking Requirement

A $25 non-refundable deposit is required at the time of booking to reserve your spot at MC Nails & Spa. This deposit will be credited directly toward your total service cost on the day of your visit.

⌛ Cancellation & Rescheduling

We require at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment. With 24+ hours' notice, your deposit can be transferred one time to a rescheduled date within 30 days. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice or no-shows forfeit the deposit.

⏰ Late Arrival Policy

We offer a 10-minute grace period. If you are running more than 10 minutes late, please call or text us immediately. Depending on our schedule, we may need to modify your requested service or reschedule to prevent overlapping with the next appointment.

💅 7-Day Quality Guarantee

We stand by our craftsmanship! We offer a complimentary touch-up within 7 days of your appointment for issues related to quality or application (such as accidental chipping, topcoat peeling, or lifting).
